# **Unit 1: Growing up**

## **Relationships**

### **1. 关系的建立与发展 (The Start of Relationships)**

1. Meet someone for the first time (初次见面)
2. **Strike up** a conversation (开始谈话)
3. Get to know someone (了解某人)
4. Form a friendship (建立友谊)
5. Build a bond (建立纽带)
6. Develop a connection (建立联系)
7. Become close (变得亲密)
8. **Hit it off (with someone)** (一见如故)
9. Make friends (with someone) (结交朋友)
10. Expand your social circle (扩大社交圈)
11. Interact with peers (与同龄人互动)
12. Establish **rapport** (建立融洽关系)
13. Find common ground (找到共同点)
14. **Bridge the gap** (消除隔阂)
15. Mutual attraction (相互吸引)

### **2. 描述关系的深度与性质 (Types & Quality)**

1. **Close-knit** family (关系紧密的家庭)
2. **Distant relative** (远房亲戚)
3. **Acquaintance** (泛泛之交)
4. Inner circle (核心朋友圈)
5. Lifelong friend (终身好友)
6. **Fair-weather friend** (酒肉朋友)
7. **Bestie** (闺蜜/死党)
8. **Confidant(e)** (知己)
9. **Role model** (榜样)
10. **Mentor-mentee relationship** (师徒关系)
11. **Sibling rivalry** (兄弟姐妹间的竞争)
12. **Platonic relationship** (柏拉图式关系)
13. Strong tie (牢固的纽带)
14. Healthy relationship (健康的关系)
15. Toxic relationship (有毒的关系)

### **3. 维护与互动 (Maintenance & Interaction)**

1. Keep in touch / Stay in touch (保持联系)
2. **Catch up with** someone (叙旧)
3. **Hang out** (消磨时间)
4. **Spend quality time** (共度时光)
5. Support each other (相互支持)
6. Value someone’s opinion (看重某人的意见)
7. Show appreciation (表示感激)
8. **Be there for someone** (支持某人)
9. Give advice (给出建议)
10. Share secrets (分享秘密)
11. Deep conversation (深度交流)
12. Mutual understanding (相互理解)
13. Trust each other (互相信任)
14. **Be on the same wavelength** (频率一致/心有灵犀)
15. **Open up to someone** (向某人敞开心扉)
